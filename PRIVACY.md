# Privacy

propozaler is an internal tool used by a two-person company to find public government solicitations.

- It reads publicly published procurement notices from government open-data sources.
- It sends a daily summary email to the two people who run it, and to no one else.
- It stores the public notice data, including the public contact details agencies publish with their notices, in a private code repository.
- It does not collect data from anyone who receives its email, does not track opens or clicks, and does not share data with third parties.
- Its Google integration is used only to send that email from the company's own Gmail account. It does not read mail.
